
The Math: Flat Galactic Rotation Curves

Standard Newtonian mechanics dictate velocity drop-offs proportional to \(1/\sqrt{r}\). This module replaces that with a naturalistic differential rotation model based on a modified Brandt galaxy configuration:
\(V(r)=\frac{V_{max}\cdot (r/R_{max})}{\left[\frac{1}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac{r}{R_{max}}\right)^{n}\right]^{3/(2n)}}\)
